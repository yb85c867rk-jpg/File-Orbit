FileOrbit stale Retry completion fix (r52)

Chosen interaction policy:
- Block Close and form replacement while Retry persistence is in flight.
- Also validate panel/form ownership after every await so forced dataset replacement/destroy can never let a late callback close another form.

Implementation:
- Panel retry captures formToken and controller before await; Close is disabled during retry; cancelEdit and openAction reject while retry is in flight.
- After await, retry validates disposed, formToken, controller identity, and appliedPending before touching controller/UI.
- Initial unified Save captures formToken/controller and validates after await before result handling.
- Panel destroy increments formToken and marks disposed; late retry/initial completion returns without controller/UI changes.
- Harness operation record now stores formToken; success/failure only clears or creates state for the originating form token.
- Conflict Save-as-copy completion validates session, dataset, and originating form token before calling panel completion.
- Retry operation has persisting flag plus panel retryInFlight, so duplicate Retry/toolbar submissions make one persistence attempt.
- PendingReservation is transferred before applied state, so Close never releases committed attachments.

Reproduction outcome:
- Retry in flight: Close control disabled; direct Close activation leaves form open.
- New form/replacement is rejected while retry is in flight.
- Forced replacement/destroy invalidates ownership; late success/failure cannot close a newer form or clear its draft.
- Successful current-owner retry closes only its form after persistence completion.
