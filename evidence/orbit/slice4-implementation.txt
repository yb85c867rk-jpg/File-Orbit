FileOrbit Orbit Slice 4 — navigation/list UI and protected writable Open

Status: implemented in the working tree. Orbit Create/Create Map/Add/rename/delete/recursion/watching/persisted handles/sync remain deferred.

Production call flow:
1. Left-rail Orbit action/Open calls orbitState.openDirectory(); OrbitDirectoryAdapter invokes showDirectoryPicker from the user click.
2. OrbitStateController enumerates top-level .map metadata; OrbitPanel renders name/status/list/current marker and Open/Refresh/Close controls.
3. Selecting a list entry reads the exact File, creates an opened-content baseline from those bytes, and calls importBackupFile with origin orbit, writable handle, document identity, openedBaseline and orbitToken.
4. importBackupFile/decodeBackup validates package content, adopts detached attachments, and reuses unsaved-map/editor guards.
5. installCreatedMap installs the authoritative tree/IDs/metadata through installCandidateEnvironment and commits activeDocument; baseline is bound at commit and post-install verification cannot replace the opened digest.
6. saveDocument (toolbar, editor Save, Retry, replacement Save-and-continue) calls protectOrbitOverwrite before encode and again before write.
7. Conflict decisions: known unchanged proceeds; changed content requires Overwrite/Save a Copy/Cancel; unverifiable content disables Overwrite; approvals are content- and destination-bound. Save a Copy invokes showSaveFilePicker from the conflict dialog click callback.
8. After write/close, createConfirmedWriteBaseline reads the actual destination and publishes only while the save/document owner is current. A baseline read failure retains savedRevision/clean state and marks the baseline unknown/observed.

Known limitations:
- Preflight-to-write remains non-atomic; no compare-and-write/rollback claim.
- Size/lastModified precheck can miss same-size/time changes; SHA-256 is stronger but not atomic.
- Native directory/file pickers, permission revocation, and pagehide behavior are not verified.
- Orbit Create/Add, rename/delete, recursion, watching, persisted handles, manifests/indexes, sync are deferred.
- Save-and-continue and Save As existing arbitrary destinations share the normal save path but are not separately native-verified.
